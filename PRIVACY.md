## 1. Xulu CLI (local software)

The published CLI is designed to run on **your machine**. For current inspect, diff, validate, and generate use, input files you point it at are processed locally by default. Those file contents are **not** uploaded to the Publisher as part of normal inspect/diff/validate/generate operation.

`xulu update` / `xulu changelog` may contact **GitHub** to fetch release metadata and binaries. That traffic is subject to GitHub’s own terms and privacy policy.

## 2. Public websites

You can browse the marketing and documentation sites without creating an account.

When you visit the sites, the hosting provider and network infrastructure typically process technical data such as IP address, browser/user-agent, requested URLs, and timestamps (server or CDN logs). This is needed to deliver the pages, keep the service secure, and diagnose faults.

Public websites may be hosted in the **EU (including Germany)** and/or on third-party platforms.

## 3. Email contact

If you email [support@xulu.dev](mailto:support@xulu.dev), we process the content of your message and your email address to respond. We do not sell this data. We keep messages only as long as needed to handle your request and any applicable legal obligations.

## 4. No sale of personal data

We do not sell your personal data.

Depending on applicable law (for example Swiss nFADP and, where it applies, GDPR), you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection. Contact [support@xulu.dev](mailto:support@xulu.dev).

Transmission over the Internet is never perfectly secure. We take reasonable steps to protect personal data.
